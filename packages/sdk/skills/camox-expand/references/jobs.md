# Jobs

Jobs that marketing pages and sections commonly do. Each entry describes what the content achieves for the visitor, never how it looks. Design every block for a job in the site's own visual language.

## Section jobs

- **Introduction**: say what the product or page is and who it is for.
- **Explanation**: show how the product works or what a feature does.
- **Benefits**: turn features into outcomes for the visitor.
- **Social proof**: show that others use and trust the product, through customers, quotes, numbers, ratings, or press.
- **Credibility**: back claims with certifications, security, compliance, or awards.
- **Objection handling**: answer the doubts that stop a visitor from acting, such as questions, risks, or "why not the alternative".
- **Comparison**: set the product against alternatives, plans, or the status quo.
- **Pricing**: say what it costs and what each option includes.
- **Conversion**: ask the visitor to act, such as sign up, buy, book, or download.
- **Lead capture**: collect contact details in exchange for something.
- **Demonstration**: let the visitor see the product working, through media, a demo, or an interactive sample.
- **Process**: walk through the steps to get started or to get a result.
- **Integration**: show what the product connects to.
- **Team and story**: introduce the people and the reasons behind the product.
- **Announcement**: flag something new or time-limited.
- **Navigation aid**: help visitors find related pages, resources, or the next step.
- **Long-form reading**: carry an article, guide, or document.

## Page jobs

- **Launch page**: present a new product or feature.
- **Comparison page**: argue for the product against a named alternative.
- **Customer story**: tell one customer's problem, solution, and results.
- **Use-case or industry page**: speak to one audience or problem.
- **Pricing page**: let visitors choose a plan.
- **Event registration**: get visitors to sign up for a webinar, meetup, or conference.
- **Changelog**: record what changed and when.
- **Resource or download page**: offer a guide, report, or template.
- **Integration page**: explain one integration.
- **About page**: explain who is behind the product.
- **Careers page**: attract applicants.
- **Contact page**: route visitors to the right channel.
- **Legal page**: carry terms, privacy, or policy text.
